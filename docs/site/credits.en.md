## Scenes in the images — `#images`

The videos shown in Tarjuman’s images are demos, prepared for these images:

- The scenes were drawn programmatically for Tarjuman, and the film titles and subtitle text were written for these images. They don’t depict real films or people.
- Tarjuman’s buttons, menus, panels and pages in the images are captures from the extension itself.
- The player bar around them in the images is a local simulation of its appearance, with no logo.
